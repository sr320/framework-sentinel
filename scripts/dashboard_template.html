Framework Sentinel

Does this paper move us? A living scorecard of incoming literature, scored against ten unresolved questions in marine invertebrate environmental epigenetics.

Sweep notes & caveats (what the most recent run actually found, verbatim)

Confirms — supports the framework's current position, or supplies first direct evidence for a claim it holds as plausible-but-untested. Complicates — introduces a nuance, boundary condition, or conflicting result. Not a negative label; these are the most valuable hits. Closes — substantially resolves a question, such that its "unresolved" framing should be revisited. Expected to be rare. Extends — applies an already-answered mechanism to a new taxon, stressor, or context without changing the answer.

The ten questions

One card per unresolved question. Staleness shows how recently the literature has said anything about it — a quiet question isn't necessarily a solved one; it may just be hard to address with current methods.

Method & cross-domain watch

Findings from outside marine invertebrate biology that are methodologically or conceptually transferable to a framework question, tagged rather than scored as if they were marine-invertebrate data.

Needs another look

On-topic hits the sweep couldn't score yet — usually because only a title, no abstract, came back. One card per item, showing its latest state; each re-check is a separate record in the log.

Sweep history

Every run of the pipeline. The log is append-only: prior scoring is never deleted or silently edited, so a corrected record appears as an annotated update alongside the original.
